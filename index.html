AI4E (Artificial Intelligence for Education) is a capacity-building initiative under Project P21 of DepEd Region VIII – Eastern Visayas.

It brings educators together to explore the knowledge, skills, and ethical practices needed to use AI meaningfully in teaching, learning, and school innovation.
